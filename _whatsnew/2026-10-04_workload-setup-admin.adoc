=== Custom dashboard updates

Custom dashboards now make it easier to manage your work.

You can edit dashboards directly from the catalog, including metrics, charts, and time ranges.

When you edit a dashboard, Workload Factory reopens the chat with your previous prompts and messages. You can then update the dashboard, save your changes, or restore the previous version.

Only the dashboard creator can edit the dashboard. While you are editing, other users cannot see your changes until you save them.

The dashboard catalog now includes more filters to help you quickly find dashboards based on category and status.

If your administrator disables AI features or your monthly credit limit is reached, dashboard creation and editing are disabled.
