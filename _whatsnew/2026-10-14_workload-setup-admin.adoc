=== Use your own Amazon Bedrock instance for AI tools

Workload Factory now includes another option on the AI settings page that lets customers use their own Amazon Bedrock instance.

This option is available under customer-managed Bedrock for AI tools and is supported by all Workload Factory AI services.

After you connect, Workload Factory lets you know whether the connection was successful.
